## Brand Spotlight

> [!example] Olipop — Welcome Flow & Retention Architecture
> **What they do:** Segment email frequency to under 2 sends per week per customer. Welcome flow is front-loaded — **90% of conversions happen on Day 0**. A single structural tweak to the Day 0 send produced a **15–22% revenue lift** in their welcome series.
>
> **Post-purchase retention:** Eli Weiss built Olipop's retention program around a "forever flavor" discovery loop — post-purchase check-in emails prompt customers to try swapping flavors within the first 30–60 days. The goal is locking in habitual repurchase before the initial novelty fades.
>
> **Results from segmentation:** Reduced send frequency → **6.9X revenue per session**, **4.9X conversion rate**, **1.4X AOV**. All flows run on Klaviyo.
>
> **What to steal:** Gate your high-frequency sends behind engagement segments. Invest heavily in Day 0 of the welcome flow — that's when purchase intent peaks. Add a flavor/variant discovery nudge in post-purchase flow emails 2–3.
>
> Sources: [Repeat.io case study](https://blog.getrepeat.io/olipops-email-campaigns-that-convert/) | [Welcome flow breakdown](https://buttondown.com/email-intel/archive/the-welcome-flow-strategy-olipop-ag1-swear-by/) | [Eli Weiss on retention](https://www.eliweisss.com/p/this-is-what-breaks-retention)

> [!example] AG1 — Welcome Flow Alignment with Olipop
> AG1 shares Olipop's Day 0 conversion philosophy. Their welcome series is optimized for Day 0 intent capture, with subsequent emails reinforcing trust via science, endorsements, and social proof rather than promotional discounts. Both brands treat the welcome flow as a retention tool, not just an acquisition close. ## Design Trends

**Interactive > Static — by a significant margin**
Interactive elements generate **2X more conversions** than static content ([Demand Metric via Litmus](https://www.litmus.com/blog/trends-in-email-marketing)). **50% of recipients** say they want to interact with content inside the email itself (Dyspatch). **97% of marketers** already used at least one interactive element in 2025 — adoption is now table stakes, not differentiation.

**What "interactive" means in practice for DTC:**
- Flavor/variant selectors inside the email
- Countdown timers for flash sales
- Accordion-style product FAQs
- One-click add-to-cart buttons (AMP email, limited client support)

**Dark mode is no longer optional**
A significant portion of subscribers keep devices in dark mode full-time. Emails not optimized for dark mode render poorly for this segment. Test both light and dark renders before every send. ([Litmus dark mode guide](https://www.litmus.com/blog/trending-in-email-design-dark-styles))

**Move away from open rate as a KPI**
Apple MPP inflates open rates by **20–40 percentage points** for brands with high iOS audiences. High-performing teams are shifting primary KPIs to revenue per email, list churn rate, and LTV. ([Litmus metrics](https://www.litmus.com/blog/email-metrics-that-matter-what-to-measure))

## Benchmarks & Data

| Metric | DTC Benchmark (2026) | Notes |
|--------|----------------------|-------|
| Open rate (segmented list) | 30–45% | Inflated by Apple MPP — treat with caution |
| Click-through rate | 2–5% | Most reliable engagement signal |
| Email-attributed revenue | 20–40% of total DTC revenue | Fully optimized programs |
| Flow revenue share | **~41%** of all email revenue | From just **5.3%** of sends |
| Flow vs. campaign CTR | **5.58% vs. 1.69%** | Flows are **3.3X** higher |
| Flow vs. campaign order rate | Flows **13X** higher | Strongest case for flow investment |
| MQL growth YoY | **+22%** | As attribution shifts toward revenue accountability |

*Sources: [Klaviyo benchmarks](https://www.klaviyo.com/products/email-marketing/benchmarks) | [Klaviyo UK blog](https://www.klaviyo.com/uk/blog/email-marketing-benchmarks-open-click-and-conversion-rates) | [Litmus State of Email](https://www.litmus.com/state-of-email-reports)*

> [!info] The flows-vs-campaigns gap is the single most under-acted-on insight in DTC email. If less than 40% of your email revenue comes from flows, that's your biggest leverage point.

## Subscription Flows

**Skio — Multi-Step Cancel Flow with Save Levers**
Skio's cancel flow now surfaces pause and shipment-delay options before the final cancel confirmation. It can also display unused store credit balances inline during the cancel sequence — a high-leverage retention tactic that recovers subscribers who have accumulated value but drifted. Customer Portal v2 supports indefinite or time-bounded pauses, configurable per brand.

**BATCH case study:** Switched to Skio and **4X'd subscription volume**, saving **$30K/month** in churn. ([Skio case study](https://skio.com/case-studies/batch-4x-d-subscription-volume-and-saves-30k-monthly-with-skio))

**Recharge — Dunning via Email + SMS**
Recharge's passive churn prevention runs automatic charge retries at configurable intervals, with email notifications on failure and optional SMS via RechargeSMS. Standard industry default is 3–4 retries over 7–14 days. ([Recharge dunning guide](https://getrecharge.com/blog/prevent-passive-churn-with-automated-recharge-dunning-features/))

> [!warning] If you're not showing subscribers their saved amount during the cancel flow, you're leaving a free retention lever on the table. Skio's portal v2 makes this a one-toggle setup.
