## Brand Spotlight

> [!example] Olipop — Winback Flow Converts Lapsed Buyers to Subscribers
> **The play:** Olipop's winback email doesn't just offer a one-time discount. It pairs a re-engagement incentive with a subscription upsell — a **15% discount on every recurring shipment** plus early flavor access. Subscribers who lapse but re-engage via this path enter a subscription, not a one-time purchase, which materially changes LTV trajectory.
>
> **Why it works:** The discount on a one-time order is table stakes in winback. Stacking a bigger benefit behind the subscription ask gives lapsed customers a reason to commit rather than cherry-pick a deal. The "early flavor access" layer adds exclusivity without margin cost.
>
> **What to steal:** Build a two-path winback: Path A = one-time discount for non-subscribers, Path B = subscription offer with stacked benefit for previous subscribers. Let Klaviyo conditional splits handle routing based on whether they've had a subscription previously. Send timing: Day 7, Day 14, Day 30 post-last-purchase.
>
> *Sources: [Repeat.io case study](https://blog.getrepeat.io/olipops-email-campaigns-that-convert/) | [Panoramata brand analysis](https://www.panoramata.co/marketing-strategy-brand/olipop)*

## Design Trends

**Mobile-First Is Non-Negotiable — 55%+ of Opens Are Mobile**
As of 2026, **55%+ of all email opens** happen on mobile devices globally. Single-column layouts, 44px minimum tap targets, and system-font stacks are table stakes — not differentiators. Where most brands still fail: hero images that render at full desktop scale on mobile, and CTAs buried below a long copy block. Rule of thumb: CTA above the fold on mobile, always. ([Klaviyo benchmarks](https://www.klaviyo.com/products/email-marketing/benchmarks))

**AI Product Recommendations Inside Flows — Clear CTR Lift**
AI-driven product recommendation blocks lift email CTR to an average of **3.75%** (vs. ~1.69% for standard campaign sends) — and top performers hit **8.79%** CTR. This is in-flow insertion, not homepage widgets. Klaviyo's native recommendations block is the fastest path; Rebuy and LoyaltyLion also integrate via custom HTML blocks. ([Klaviyo UK blog](https://www.klaviyo.com/uk/blog/email-marketing-benchmarks-open-click-and-conversion-rates))

## Subscription Flows

> [!warning] Recharge Acquires Skio — April 30, 2026
> **The deal:** Recharge acquired Skio for **$105M cash**. Skio was at **$32M ARR**, processing ~$4B in payments annually. Combined entity: **20,000+ merchants**, **$20B+ GMV**. ([Recharge blog](https://getrecharge.com/blog/recharge-welcomes-skio-to-build-the-future-of-subscription-commerce/) | [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/recharge-welcomes-skio-to-build-the-future-of-subscription-commerce-302759194.html))
>
> **What changes now:** Nothing immediately. Both platforms continue operating independently, and existing account teams remain points of contact.
>
> **What to watch (12–24 month window):**
> - Pricing harmonization (Skio's model was simpler/cheaper at scale)
> - Feature set consolidation — Skio's Customer Portal v2 cancel-flow UX was a key differentiator
> - Support quality as teams integrate
>
> **If you're on Skio:** Don't migrate reactively. Watch the next 2–3 product update cycles. If Skio's cancel-flow configurability or pricing structure degrades, that's the trigger to evaluate Loop, Bold, or a headless solution.
>
> **If you're evaluating platforms:** Loop Subscriptions is now the most credible independent alternative for Shopify brands. ([Loop blog](https://www.loopwork.co/blog/recharge-payments-vs-skio-subscriptions))

**Smart Dunning — The Gap Between Basic and Advanced Recovery**
Standard dunning (3–4 retries over 7–14 days) recovers **15–25%** of failed payments. Smart dunning — routing retry timing and channel by decline code, card type, customer segment, and bank behavior — recovers **55–80%**. Failed payments drive **20–40% of total subscription churn**. If your dunning setup is "retry 3 times, send email, cancel," you have room to recover significant revenue. ([Churn Buster](https://churnbuster.io/articles/best-dunning-management-software))

## Action Items
- [ ] Assess your subscription platform situation if on Skio — document what features matter most before any integration announcement changes the calculus
- [ ] Build a two-path winback flow: one-time discount offer vs. subscription upsell with stacked benefit (see Olipop tactic above)
- [ ] Add an AI product recommendations block to your post-purchase flow — target the Day 7–14 send where cross-sell intent is highest
- [ ] Audit mobile rendering on your top 5 flow emails: CTA above the fold? Single-column layout? 44px tap targets?
- [ ] If on WooCommerce + Klaviyo: explore Customer Hub rollout for account dashboard and retention banner use cases
- [ ] Pull your flow RPR in Klaviyo — if it's below $3, your segmentation or content relevance is the bottleneck, not send timing
