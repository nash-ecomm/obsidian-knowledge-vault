## Klaviyo Updates

### Composer — AI Campaign Builder (Private Beta)
Klaviyo launched [Composer](https://www.klaviyo.com/composer), a prompt-to-campaign AI agent that generates a complete send from a single natural-language instruction: subject line, preview text, body copy, images, audience segment, and flow logic. It draws on **14+ years of performance data across 193,000 Klaviyo brands** to make channel, timing, and copy decisions — not just generic LLM outputs. Campaign builds that previously took a day can be prototyped in minutes. Currently in private beta; join the waitlist from the Klaviyo dashboard. Sources: [Klaviyo Composer page](https://www.klaviyo.com/composer), [Klaviyo newsroom](https://www.klaviyo.com/newsroom/composer).

> [!info] What Composer Is and Isn't
> Composer is strongest for standard lifecycle flows (welcome, abandon, win-back) where the performance data is deep. Treat its output as a high-quality first draft, not a launch-ready asset — brand voice, offer logic, and suppression rules still require human review. Don't use it unsupervised for high-stakes sends (VIP, reactivation) until you've validated its output against your benchmarks.

The [Customer Agent](https://www.klaviyo.com/whats-new) now handles **order edits, returns, subscription changes, and loyalty lookups** — full transactional support, not just FAQ deflection. Brands using this effectively can deflect a significant share of CS tickets while keeping subscribers in self-service flows rather than canceling. ## Brand Spotlight

> [!example] Olipop — Loyalty-First Email Strategy
> Olipop's email program is built around low frequency and high relevance: **fewer than 2 emails/week**, with subject lines anchored to seasonal moments and new flavor drops. The acquisition hook is a **20% off first order** incentive; retention is built around a loyalty program that awards **150 points on email signup**. Every email list entry is a loyalty program entry — meaning subscribers are pre-enrolled in a retention mechanic before they've even purchased. The ESP is Klaviyo.
>
> **What to steal:**
> - Gate the loyalty signup behind email capture — subscribers have higher retention intent than anonymous visitors, so the 150-point welcome bonus costs you almost nothing on CLV math
> - Keep campaign frequency at ≤2/week and invest the saved bandwidth into subject line testing — Olipop's seasonal hooks ("🍊 Summer's first drop is here") consistently outperform product-feature subject lines
> - Use emoji strategically in subject lines for beverage/CPG categories — **90%+ of Olipop's sends include emoji**, correlating with above-average open rates in their vertical
>
> Sources: [Repeat.io — Olipop Email Campaigns](https://blog.getrepeat.io/olipops-email-campaigns-that-convert/), [Ecommerce Fastlane — Olipop Retention Strategy](https://ecommercefastlane.com/olipops-best-in-class-customer-retention-strategy-let-them-lead-the-way/).

## Design Trends

Per [Litmus — Email Marketing Trends 2026](https://www.litmus.com/blog/trends-in-email-marketing) and [Designmodo — Email Design Trends 2026](https://designmodo.com/email-design-trends/):

**Accessibility is moving from checkbox to design-first.** Brands building accessibility into templates from the start (semantic structure, sufficient contrast, descriptive alt text, tap targets ≥44px) are seeing meaningfully better engagement from the ~15% of email users who rely on assistive technology — a segment that over-indexes on loyalty and AOV. This is not a compliance exercise; it's a conversion lever that compounds.

**Dark mode optimization is underweight in most DTC programs.** Apple Mail, Gmail app, and Outlook now render dark mode for **~50% of email opens** — but most DTC templates are built and tested light-only. A targeted dark mode audit (logo handling, CTA button contrast, background hex fallback) takes one sprint and eliminates the rendering gap for half your list.

> [!warning] The AMP Conversion Stat to Sanity-Check
> A widely-circulated claim that AMP in-email bookings boost conversions "up to 300%" appears to originate from vendor marketing, not controlled study data. Until Litmus or Klaviyo publish platform-level AMP conversion data, treat this number as directional. The better metric: AMP increases *clicks* because it reduces steps — but Apple Mail still doesn't support it, capping coverage at ~40% of most DTC lists.

## Benchmarks & Data

Sources: [Klaviyo 2026 Email Benchmarks](https://www.klaviyo.com/products/email-marketing/benchmarks), [MHI Growth Engine — DTC Email Benchmarks 2026](https://mhigrowthengine.com/blog/dtc-email-benchmarks-2026/).

**Open rate is no longer a reliable primary KPI.** Apple's Mail Privacy Protection pre-loads tracking pixels regardless of actual opens — inflating reported rates across the board. Sophisticated DTC programs have shifted to **revenue per subscriber per month (RPS)** as the north-star metric.

| Metric | DTC Average (Segmented) | Top-Decile DTC | Notes |
|--------|------------------------|----------------|-------|
| Open rate (reported) | 30–45% | 50%+ | Inflated by Apple MPP |
| Click-through rate | 2–5% | 6%+ | Most reliable engagement signal |
| Click-to-open rate (CTOR) | 6–8% | 12%+ | Use CTOR over CTR for content quality |
| Email-attributed revenue | 20–30% of store revenue | 35–40% | Flows + campaigns combined |
| Revenue per recipient (RPR) — top flows | $0.40–$1.20 | $3–$7+ | Welcome, abandon, win-back |

**Industry-wide average** (all sectors, 183K+ Klaviyo customers): open rate **43.46%**, CTR **2.09%**, CTOR **6.81%**. DTC e-commerce typically runs below the all-industry open rate average but above on CTR when segmentation is healthy.

> [!tip] The Metric Shift in Practice
> Instead of reporting "our open rate is 38%," report "our RPS is $X and we're targeting $Y by Q3." Build your weekly email report around: RPS trend, CTOR by flow, unsubscribe rate by segment. These three metrics tell you whether content resonates, segmentation is working, and list health is directionally healthy.

## Subscription Flows

### Recharge Acquires Skio — What It Means Now
Recharge completed the acquisition of Skio for **$105M in cash** (Skio had raised only $8M total and was at **$32M ARR**, a remarkable capital efficiency story). Combined platform: **20,000+ brands, $20B+ GMV annually**. Sources: [Recharge blog](https://getrecharge.com/blog/recharge-welcomes-skio-to-build-the-future-of-subscription-commerce/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/y-combinator-alum-skio-sells-for-105m-cash-only-raised-8m-founder-says/), [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/recharge-welcomes-skio-to-build-the-future-of-subscription-commerce-302759194.html).

**Current operational status:** both platforms continue running independently with no forced migrations. Existing teams remain points of contact. A combined roadmap will be published in the months ahead.

**What the combined platform will eventually do:** benchmark retention against comparable brands in your category, track how checkout revenue trends month-over-month, and surface exactly where to close retention gaps — positioning it as a subscription intelligence layer, not just a billing tool.

> [!warning] Migration Hold
> If you're currently evaluating a switch between Recharge and Skio, **hold the decision** until the combined roadmap is public. Migrating to Skio today means migrating into a product that will change materially within 12 months. Migrating away from Recharge may be premature if the combined roadmap addresses your friction points. Re-evaluate in Q3.

### Dunning Best Practice: Front-Load Retries, Back-Load Emails
The highest-leverage dunning shift per [Churnbuster — Dunning Best Practices](https://churnbuster.io/dunning-best-practices): **separate payment retries from email sends**. The optimal sequence is to attempt 2–3 retries silently before triggering any customer communication. For insufficient funds specifically, retries timed near **payday windows (1st and 15th)** recover significantly more without requiring customer action. Reserve email for expired card failures — those never recover from retries alone and need an immediate update-card nudge.
