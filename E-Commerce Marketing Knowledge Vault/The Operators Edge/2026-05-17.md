# The Operators Edge — May 17, 2026

> [!tip] Top Takeaway
> Poppi went from $500K (Shark Tank 2018) to $500M+ (2024) to a $1.95B PepsiCo exit by turning its founder into a TikTok-native media brand. ## Deep Dive: Poppi — The $1.95B Founder-as-Brand Playbook

Poppi's origin story reads like a controlled experiment in narrative compounding: Allison and Stephen Ellsworth were making apple cider vinegar tonics in their Dallas kitchen when Allison appeared on *Shark Tank* in 2018, nine months pregnant, as "Mother Beverage" — and walked out with **$400K from Rohan Oza (CAVU Consumer Partners) for 25% equity** ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/04/17/poppi-cofounder-allison-ellsworth-shark-tank-pitch-while-pregnant-2-billion-dollar-brand/)). At the time, the company had roughly $500K in revenue. The rebrand followed: colorful aluminum cans instead of glass bottles, a new name built for shelf visibility and social shareability, and a repositioning from "wellness supplement" to "fun soda with functional benefits." The visual identity was redesigned to win a decision in a TikTok scroll — not a grocery aisle.

The revenue trajectory from there is one of the sharpest in modern DTC: **$13M in 2020 → $500M+ in 2024** — a **38x increase in four years** ([Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/building-a-business/she-sold-poppi-to-pepsico-for-1-95-billion)). The engine was Allison's personal TikTok presence. Rather than building a brand account with polished production, Ellsworth became a creator: founder stories, taste tests, behind-the-scenes operations, relatable memes. When she posted a TikTok explaining her Shark Tank deal, it generated **$100,000 in sales in 24 hours**. That signal locked in the strategy — TikTok-first, founder-fronted, authenticity over production value. By 2024, Poppi was sold in **120+ retailers including Whole Foods and Target**, but the brand equity that justified those shelf placements was built entirely on social-native content. Coca-Cola's launch of **Simply Pop in February 2025** validated the category urgency, and PepsiCo closed the acquisition for **$1.95B** in May 2025 — a **3.9x revenue multiple** that reflected both category momentum and the scarcity of DTC brands with genuine consumer pull at scale ([PepsiCo](https://www.pepsico.com/our-stories/press-release/pepsico-to-acquire-poppi03172025); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/03/17/pepsico-buys-prebiotic-soda-brand-poppi-for-more-than-1point6-billion.html)).

The cautionary footnote: Poppi's **Super Bowl 2025 influencer campaign**. The brand sent custom-built vending machines — estimated at ~$25K each — to 32 creators ahead of the Super Bowl. The intended outcome was organic creator content that looked like genuine gifting. The actual outcome was a TikTok firestorm: consumers and smaller creators accused Poppi of being tone-deaf for "sending expensive gifts to already-rich influencers" rather than communities, hospitals, or schools. Competitor **Olipop capitalized immediately** — responding publicly to comments, speculating on campaign costs, and shipping their own counterprogramming ([Marketing Brew](https://www.marketingbrew.com/stories/2025/02/13/poppi-olipop-super-bowl-vending-machine-campaign-controversy); [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/annabel-burba/poppi-fumbled-its-super-bowl-marketing-move-competitor-olipop-went-on-the-offensive/91145733)). The structural lesson: large-scale influencer gifting programs are effectively public activations — the optics of *who* receives what and *why* are as important as the content generated. The vending machine stunt did not derail the PepsiCo exit, but it briefly negated years of authentic founder-brand equity.

> [!tip] Takeaway
> Founder content is the highest-leverage organic channel in DTC — but it only compounds if it's genuinely authentic. Allison's Shark Tank video worked because it was transparent, not because it was polished. If your founder has a genuine story about why the product exists, a series of five unscripted TikToks telling it will outperform your next brand video shoot.

> [!warning] Influencer Gifting Optics Rule
> Before any large-scale gifting activation, ask: *How does this look if a critic screenshots it?* Poppi's vending machines were defensible on cost — but the perception of "expensive gifts to famous people" destroyed weeks of goodwill. Test gifting logic against your least-sympathetic audience first.

## Deep Dive: Caraway Home — Design-First Cookware and the Creator Flywheel

Caraway launched in 2019 with a thesis that Jordan Nathan has articulated clearly: cookware was a functional category designed exclusively for function, and no brand had yet competed on design, color, and home aesthetics. The product line — non-toxic ceramic-coated cookware in a curated palette of colors — was engineered to be purchased by someone who cares how their kitchen looks as much as what they cook in it. The entry set includes a modular lid storage system and a pot rack: organizational solutions that happen to also be beautiful and highly photographable. **Packaging is a content format.** Every Caraway product is unboxing-native ([Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/blog/caraway-cookware-stand-out-marketing)).

The influencer strategy that followed was counterintuitive: Caraway explicitly did not target food creators or chefs. They went after **lifestyle, home decor, and interiors influencers** — people whose audiences follow for aesthetics, not recipes. The reasoning is tight: a chef's audience evaluates cookware on performance, where Caraway competes against All-Clad and Le Creuset. A home-and-lifestyle creator's audience evaluates it on design, where Caraway has no competition in its price band. The program started with **35 ambassadors** and scaled to **3,000+ creators** over five years. The ambassador program now drives **13% of Caraway's total revenue**, with approximately **$350K paid in affiliate commissions in 2022 alone** ([Kynship](https://www.kynship.co/blog/zero-to-3-500-ambassadors-caraways-influencer-marketing-program-driving-explosive-growth); [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/starting-a-business/how-caraway-non-toxic-cookware-grew-500-in-just-four-years/477357)). The compounding tactic: high-performing UGC gets pulled into paid social, where it outperforms traditional brand creative — delivering higher **average order value** than standard paid media conversions.

Caraway's channel evolution mirrors a mature DTC-to-retail arc. The brand has sold into more than **2.5 million households since launch** and is now available in **~1,000 retail stores plus Amazon**. But CEO Jordan Nathan frames retail as an **acquisition channel**, not a distribution outlet — particularly for registries and gifting moments (weddings, housewarmings) that bring entirely new households into the brand. "We very much see retailers as acquisition channels," he's stated explicitly ([Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/retailers/we-very-much-see-retailers-as-acquisition-channels-caraway-ceo-jordan-nathan-on-the-cookware-brands-growth-strategy/)). 2025 brought the brand's first major OOH campaign — a "Made Better" tagline across out-of-home placements, shifting creative from product-only imagery (the historical Facebook strategy) to lifestyle photography with real people cooking. The company set **"impossible to ignore"** as its 2025 brand north star ([Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/marketing/brands-briefing-caraway-makes-its-first-big-ooh-push-as-it-seeks-to-make-the-brand-impossible-to-ignore/)), representing the transition from DTC performance brand to full-funnel brand investment.

> [!tip] Takeaway
> Caraway's influencer selection principle is more important than the program size: **match creator audience identity to the buyer identity**, not product category. If you sell a product that lives in a kitchen, bathroom, or closet, the creator who covers *the room* often outperforms the creator who covers *the use case*. The design story is the acquisition story.

> [!example] The UGC-to-Paid-Ads Bridge
> Caraway's flywheel: seed product → creator posts organically → top-performing posts get licensed and run as paid social creative → those ads drive higher AOV than brand-produced content because the social proof is embedded in the creative format. Build this bridge deliberately: identify your top 3 UGC creators monthly, license their content within 30 days of posting, and A/B test against brand video in your next campaign.

## Quick Headlines

**DTC M&A Multiple Benchmark — What Poppi's Exit Signals:** Poppi's **3.9x trailing revenue multiple** ($1.95B on $500M revenue) is the clearest recent data point on what a strategic acquirer pays for a DTC brand with genuine consumer pull, multi-channel distribution, and category leadership. For context: most DTC brands that sell to PE trade at **1–2x revenue**; strategics pay the premium only when the brand has organic velocity, retail proof, and a defensible acquisition channel they can't easily replicate. The Poppi comp suggests that **brand equity + retail velocity + social proof-of-demand** is the triad that unlocks strategic premiums. Build toward that exit thesis from year one, not year seven ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/03/17/pepsico-buys-prebiotic-soda-brand-poppi-for-more-than-1point6-billion.html); [Food Dive](https://www.fooddive.com/news/pepsico-buys-poppi-2B-pepsi-purchase-prebiotic-healthy-soda-better-for-you-drink-olipop-simply-pop/742656/)).

## Action Items
- [ ] **Audit your founder's content presence**: Is the founder showing up authentically on TikTok or Reels with unscripted content? Assign 2 posts/week minimum — no production budget required
- [ ] **Reclassify your influencer program by audience identity, not product category**: Identify 5 creators in adjacent lifestyle categories (home, fitness, travel) whose audiences match your buyer — not just creators who review products in your vertical
- [ ] **Build the UGC-to-paid bridge**: License the top 3 pieces of creator content from the past 30 days and A/B test against your current best-performing paid creative — compare AOV, not just CTR
- [ ] **Run an influencer gifting optics check**: Before any large-scale gifting activation, ask "how does this read to someone who didn't receive it?" — Poppi's vending machine lesson applies to any PR send at scale
- [ ] **Calculate your DTC exit multiple readiness**: Use Poppi's 3.9x as a strategic benchmark — map where you are on the three levers that justified the premium: organic social velocity, retail proof-of-demand, and a defensible acquisition channel
